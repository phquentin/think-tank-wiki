Think-tank wiki

A knowledge base the agent writes and you can check.

Chat answers evaporate, and every new question starts from the raw documents again. This wiki keeps what was learned as linked markdown pages, every claim cited back to its source, so each session starts from the accumulated state.

1 · Three layers

Sources stay untouched. Pages are derived. Rules are written down.

  • raw/ is what came in: documents, fetched articles, your notes. The agent reads it and never changes it.
  • wiki/ is what we know: pages the agent writes from raw, each claim citing back.
  • AGENTS.md is how the agent must behave: page format, workflows, hard rules. It reads it at the start of every session.
2 · Two roles

You curate. The agent edits.

  • You bring sources, state what you know, make decisions, ask questions, and approve what gets fetched.
  • The agent reads, writes pages, links them, cites every claim, and logs every change.

One rule: you never hand-edit wiki/. Changes go through chat, so the index, the log, and the catalogs stay consistent.

3 · Getting things in

Three doors into raw/.

  • Drop a file. A report, a paper, a scan. Sensitive originals go in a gitignored folder; the digest still gets written.
  • /research. The agent searches, shows a ranked list of candidates, you pick, it fetches with provenance.
  • /note. A fact you know, saved with date and confidence. Later cited as per user, never mistaken for a paper.
4 · /ingest

One source in. One digest out. The pages it touches updated.

  • Read it in full, discuss the takeaways, then write one source page: TLDR, key claims, caveats.
  • Update the entity, concept, and hub pages it bears on. At most 10–15 pages per source.
  • Close the open questions it answers. Update the index. Append to the log.

Idempotent: running it twice on the same file changes nothing.

5 · Inside a page

Every claim says what it is and where it came from.

  • [fact] stated by a source, cited
  • [inference] the agent's synthesis, says what it rests on
  • [decision] a constraint you set
  • [open-question] unknown, with the action that closes it

Disagreements are kept visible. Sources that conflict get a Contradictions section. The wiki's own mistakes are corrected in place under a dated note, never silently rewritten.

6 · /query

Answers come from the wiki, with citations, or not at all.

  • Reads the index, then the relevant pages, and answers with links to pages and raw files.
  • If the wiki is thin on the topic, it says so and proposes /research, /note, or /decide instead of improvising.
  • Your decisions filter the recommendations. Reusable answers are filed back as synthesis pages, the next step.
7 · Synthesis

Where it comes together, and what you actually read.

  • Answer pages. A /query or /research conclusion filed back, every claim linking to the pages and sources it rests on. Written once, cited from then on.
  • Hub pages. Living trackers: a timeline, an inventory of what was tried, a record of what was checked. Every ingest that touches them updates them.

Entities and concepts hold the facts. Synthesis holds the argument. Nobody re-reads 180 sources to find out where things stand.

8 · Bookkeeping

An index, a log, and two catalogs keep it navigable.

  • index.md: one line per page, read first on every question.
  • log.md: append-only. ## [2026-08-25] ingest | eight folate sources
  • /lint: a health check for contradictions, orphans, and unsourced claims. It regenerates open-questions.md and todos.md from the markers on the pages.
9 · It compounds

Each answer leaves the wiki better than it found it.

A question exposes a gap, research fills it, the ingest links it to what was already there, and the answer raises the next question. Nothing is re-derived.
